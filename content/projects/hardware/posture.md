We won Best Overall at Cornell Makeathon 2026 for the prompt intelligent wearables. I had an incredibly fun time, I somehow knew basically everyone participating, or I got to know them over the course of the event, and I got lots of fun stickers. We also won Best Overall! Pretty swanky. We designed an automatic posture corrector, based in two prongs of behavior change: automatic feedback & correction + attachment to identity & pride. We placed four pressure sensors on various locations on the back, testing to figure out which configurations would be most optimal, and would interact best with the harness we'd made, then we wrote algorithms to determine when people were slouching.
