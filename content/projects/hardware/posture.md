We won Best Overall at Cornell Makeathon 2026 for the prompt intelligent wearables. I had an incredibly fun time, I somehow knew basically everyone participating, or I got to know them over the course of the event, and I got lots of fun stickers. We also won Best Overall! Pretty swanky. We designed an automatic posture corrector, based in two prongs of behavior change: automatic feedback & correction + attachment to identity & pride. We placed four pressure sensors on various locations on the back, testing to figure out which configurations would be most optimal, and would interact best with the harness we'd made, then we wrote algorithms to determine when people were slouching. This then triggered a buzzer, located between the scapula, to automatically jolt your back into the right position. This was also connected to a bluetooth module that connected to a realtime firebase leaderboard, which kept track of how long you were slouching relative to everyone else participating. 

Overall very fun! I got to assemble our team, and I worked on prototyping, design, electronics, and presentation. Learned so much about electronics. And I thought it was really effective, given that I was the test monkey for the hackathon, and my posture actually ended up improving.
